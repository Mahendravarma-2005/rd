Important Next Steps

To complete your fee reimbursement request, please read and acknowledge these guidelines:

  • Print Document: After accepting, download or print the generated Cover Letter & Application Form.
  • Obtain Signatures: Get physical signatures from the Student, the Faculty Guide, and the HOD (Head of Department) on the printed copy.
  • Attach Enclosures: Enclose the Xerox copy of your bank passbook (first page), the published paper's first page, and your original Registration Fee Receipt.
  • R&D Office: Submit the completed, signed physical packet to the R&D Cell for final approval.
  • Deadline: The student should submit the physical copy to the R&D Cell within one college working day.
