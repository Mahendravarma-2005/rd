# Vignan University Assets Directory

Place your custom university images and logos here:

- **Campus Backgrounds**: Add your campus photo as `vignan-campus.jpg` or `vignan-campus.png` in this `images/` directory. The stylesheet automatically prioritizes your custom image.
- **Logos & Crests**: Add your university crest or department logo as `vignan-logo.png` in `assets/logos/`.

### Default Included Assets:
- `vignan-campus-bg.svg` - High-resolution architectural campus backdrop with subtle ambient lighting.
- `vignan-crest.svg` - Official style gold & navy VFSTR university seal.
